About and settings

Privacy. Nothing is saved. Every entry lives in memory and is discarded when you close or reload the app.

Report logo. The report uses a placeholder mark. Pick the clinic logo image to use it on reports for this session.

Sources. Each score card lists its original publication. Coefficients come from the published papers or from open implementations checked against the papers' worked examples. PRECISE-DAPT, SYNTAX II, STS-PROM, SMART2 and SCORE2-Diabetes are not included because their coefficients could not be verified.

Install on iPhone. Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Disclaimer. Decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgement. Confirm each score applies to the patient in front of you.
